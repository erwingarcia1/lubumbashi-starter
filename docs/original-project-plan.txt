           MUNICIPAL TAX COLLECTION PLATFORM
                                        Developer Project Plan & Development Roadmap


   Objective                    Build a secure digital platform to identify and geolocate taxpayers, assess municipal taxes/fees, collect
                                electronic payments, issue verifiable receipts, reconcile transactions and monitor revenue in real time.

   Core users                   Municipal administrators, finance officers, supervisors, auditors, field/census agents and taxpayers.

   Target MVP                   Pilot-ready platform in approximately 14 weeks, followed by field testing and progressive deployment.



 1. Project Objective
 The platform must create a reliable municipal revenue management system, not only a payment app. It should
 support taxpayer census, identification, geolocation, assessment, collection, payment verification, reconciliation,
 reporting and audit. The architecture must allow additional municipal taxes and fees to be added later without
 rebuilding the system.

 2. Main Components
 A. Field Agent / TPE Application
   • Secure agent login and role permissions.

   • Register individuals, companies, businesses, properties and taxable activities.

   • Capture phone, identification details, address, commune/zone, category, GPS coordinates and photographs.

   • Generate a unique taxpayer ID and QR code.

   • Scan QR codes and view taxes due, outstanding balances and payment history.

   • Initiate electronic payments and issue digital receipts after confirmed payment.

   • Support offline census operations with later synchronization.

   • Agents must never be able to manually mark a transaction as PAID.

 B. Municipal Administration Dashboard
   • Show registered taxpayers, amount assessed, amount collected, outstanding balances and collection rate.

   • Analyze collections by day/week/month/year, tax type, commune/zone, agent and payment channel.

   • Display a GPS map of taxpayers and collection coverage.

   • Show unpaid taxpayers, failed/cancelled transactions, agent performance and suspicious activity.

   • Export financial and operational reports to Excel/PDF.

   • Support roles such as Administrator, Mayor, Finance, Supervisor, Auditor and Field Agent.

 C. Payment & Reconciliation System
   • Integrate M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Orange Money, bank/card TPE and future payment providers through APIs.

   • Use a unique transaction reference for every payment.

   • Confirm payment only after successful server/provider confirmation.

   • Handle failed, delayed, duplicated and reversed transactions safely.

   • Perform automatic daily reconciliation between provider records and the platform.

 3. Taxpayer Census & Digital Identity
 Each taxpayer, property, business or taxable activity should receive a unique municipal digital identity. The data
 model must allow one taxpayer to own or operate multiple taxable entities.

                                                    Example ID: LUB-KAM-00000125
 Core relationship: Taxpayer → Business/Property → GPS → Category → Tax obligations → QR Code → Payments
 → Outstanding amounts → History

 4. Configurable Tax & Fee Engine
   • Tax/fee name and taxpayer category.

   • Fixed amount or percentage-based calculation.

   • Monthly, quarterly, annual or configurable frequency.

   • Due dates, exemptions, penalties and effective dates.

   • Administrative configuration without changing application source code.

 5. QR Code & Geolocation
 QR scanning should immediately identify the taxpayer/entity and retrieve location, tax category, obligations, payment
 history and outstanding balance. Receipts should also contain a verification QR code. GPS data should support
 mapping of registered, paid, unpaid, unvisited and potentially duplicated locations.

 6. Security & Anti-Fraud
   • Role-based access control and strong authentication.

   • Encrypted communications and secure API access.

   • Complete immutable audit trail for sensitive actions.

   • Device identification, GPS and time stamping.

   • Duplicate taxpayer and duplicate payment detection.

   • Server-side payment verification and controlled reversals.

   • Backups, recovery procedures and session controls.

   • Record WHO + WHAT + WHEN + WHERE + DEVICE for critical actions.
